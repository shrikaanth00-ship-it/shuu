Atmospheric Knowledge & Guide Center

Scientific thresholds, hardware reference parameters, and biological health advisories

01
Atmospheric Index Standard
Air quality index metrics communicate pollution levels to define risk factors. Good air quality represents clean, toxin-free air, whereas elevated levels indicate dangerous atmospheric concentrations that trigger physical health risks.
02
MQ-2 Gas Sensor Interface
The MQ-2 is a semiconductor gas sensor sensitive to LPG, propane, hydrogen, methane, carbon monoxide, and smoke. The sensing layer consists of a tin dioxide (SnO₂) element. In clean air, the conductivity of SnO₂ is low. When exposed to target gases, the sensor's resistance decreases, generating an analog signal translated into parts per million (PPM).
Threshold RangeDiagnosticsAdvisory Action Required
0 – 200 PPMNominalNormal conditions; no mitigation necessary.
200 – 500 PPMModerate AlertSensitive demographics should monitor closely.
500 – 1000 PPMElevated WarningVentilate interior spaces immediately.
1000+ PPMHazard AlertEvacuate structure; emergency ventilation recommended.
03
Microclimate Metrics
The DHT11 sensor measures ambient temperature and relative humidity. Indoor temperature and humidity are critical parameters; high humidity values promote micro-organic growth and allergen concentrations, while excessive temperatures reduce pulmonary comfort.
Meteorological ParameterTarget Comfort ZoneCritical Threshold
Temperature Index20 – 25°CExceeding 35°C or Below 10°C
Relative Humidity40 – 60%Exceeding 70% or Below 30%
04
European Air Quality Index Scale
The OpenWeatherMap Air Pollution API integrates major atmospheric particulate levels (PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, O₃, SO₂, and CO) and indexes the results into five health categories.
AQI IndexSafety ClassificationHealth Implication Summary
1ExcellentNo risk factors detected.
2SatisfactoryAcceptable limits for the general population.
3Moderate AlertMinor discomfort for highly sensitive groups.
4Unhealthy WarningPotential health risks for the general public.
5Hazard WarningIncreased susceptibility to respiratory irritation.
05
System Architecture (IoT)
The hardware utilizes a dual-core 240MHz ESP32 microchip. Readings are sampled locally every 2 seconds, pushed to ThingSpeak cloud via HTTP requests every 20 seconds, and visualised client-side using REST APIs.
06
Pulmonary & Health Effects
Exposure to elevated toxic gases or particulate levels triggers dry throats, coughs, and lightheadedness. Chronic exposure is linked to long-term pulmonary conditions and decreased lung function.

ACADEMIC RESEARCH INITIATIVE

AirWatch Diagnostics

A real-time environmental monitoring platform utilizing an ESP32 microcontroller, DHT11 microclimate sensor, and MQ-2 chemical sensor array. Pushes continuous telemetry logs to a cloud-based ThingSpeak channel for real-time analytics.
